# Stroke data sources

The JSON in this folder is generated by `pnpm seed:script-models`
(`scripts/generateScriptModels.mjs`). Do not edit it by hand — change
`scripts/characterSets.mjs` and regenerate.

## Make Me a Hanzi

Chinese characters, and the fallback for Han characters used in Japanese, come
from [Make Me a Hanzi](https://github.com/skishore/makemeahanzi) via the
[`hanzi-writer-data`](https://github.com/chanind/hanzi-writer-data) package.

The graphics data is derived from the Arphic PL fonts and is distributed under
the Arphic Public License; the accompanying data is MIT licensed.

## KanjiVG

Japanese kana and kanji stroke data comes from
[KanjiVG](https://kanjivg.tagaini.net), copyright © Ulrich Apel, distributed
under the [Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 3.0
licence](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/).

The stroke paths here are a derivative work: KanjiVG's SVG curves are sampled
into point lists and rescaled to a 1024 grid. That derivative is shared under
the same CC BY-SA 3.0 terms.
